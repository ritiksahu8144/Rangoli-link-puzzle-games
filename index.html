Rangoli Link. Join matching dots, never cross a line, and light up the rangoli.

How to play

Drag from a dot to the dot of the same colour.

Lines can’t cross. Dragging over another line cuts it.

Link every pair and fill every tile to win. Solve without hints for 3 stars. Finish a level to open the next one.

Drag from a dot to its match. Link every pair and fill every tile to win.
